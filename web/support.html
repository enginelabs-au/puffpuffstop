Support

How the app
fits together.

PuffPuffStop is a 16+ wellness habit coach for logging puffs, keeping a daily goal, and watching motivational estimates for your lungs, heart, brain, liver, and mouth. Organ scores are estimates, not diagnoses.

Set your plan

Onboarding and Settings ask, in order, how many puffs you use, how many you want to reduce by over a period, the daily puff goal you want to reach, and when you want to reach zero puffs. The daily goal is the number of puffs per day from the day you set it. Home shows that goal.

Log and undo

Tap Log to record a puff. Undo removes the latest puff from today. You can also log with this phone’s voice assistant.

Pacing and reminders

Hourly pacing splits the daily goal across the day. Optional reminders stay on this device: a daily check-in, and a notice when a pacing window still has unused puffs. Turn them on or off in Settings.

Progress and savings

Stats shows your puffs over time. The savings pot estimates money from puffs you did not use, using the device cost you enter. It does not hold or pay out money.

Health

The health app on this phone is optional. If you allow it, the app can read heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, and blood oxygen around a log. That data stays on this device. It is context, not a diagnosis.

Export or delete

Settings can export a JSON copy of the data on this device, or delete that local data. Deleting clears the plan, logs, and settings stored on the phone. What is kept, and what is left out of an export, is described in the privacy policy.

If you want more help

The app is a habit log, not a clinic. If you want extra support to quit, talk to your GP or call Quitline on 13 7848. If you need urgent medical help, contact local emergency services.
