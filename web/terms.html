Who this app is for

PuffPuffStop is a 16+ wellness habit coach for logging puffs, keeping a daily goal, and viewing motivational estimates. It is not a kids app, not for anyone under 16, and not a medical device. Organ scores are motivational estimates, not diagnoses. The app does not promise that you will quit.

What you get

This version stores your plan, logs, settings, and estimated puff savings on this device. It does not create an account, show ads, process cards, hold money, or pay out money. The savings figure is an estimate from the device cost you enter. Optional reminders stay on this device. Connecting the health app on this phone is optional, and watch readings are wellness context, not a diagnosis.

Your data

You can export a JSON copy of the data on this device, or delete that local data, from Settings. Deleting clears the plan, logs, and settings stored on the phone. Details are in the privacy policy.

Acceptable use

Use the app for your own habit tracking. Do not rely on it for medical, clinical, or emergency decisions. If you need medical help, contact a qualified clinician or local emergency services.
