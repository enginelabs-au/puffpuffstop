Privacy

Your log stays
on your phone.

This policy explains what PuffPuffStop keeps, where it stays, and the choices you have. It covers the app and this website.

Effective 22 November 2026

Who this app is for

PuffPuffStop is a 16+ wellness habit coach. It is not a kids app, not for anyone under 16, and not a medical device. Organ scores are motivational estimates, not diagnoses.

Who operates it

PuffPuffStop is published by Engine Labs in Australia. This policy is the public privacy notice for the app and for puffpuffstop.com.

What stays on this device

Your nickname, plan answers, daily puff log (counts and local log times), goal-day history, settings, and estimated puff savings stay on this device as a local snapshot. This build does not upload that data.

The snapshot is there so the app can show your goal, your log, pacing, and the savings estimate after you close it. Deleting the app, or using Delete in Settings, removes that local copy.

Health and watches

Connecting the health app on this phone is optional. If you allow it, and a watch shares them, we keep heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, and blood oxygen around a puff log. That context stays on this device.

Watch data is optional wellness context, not a diagnosis. We only read Health types you approve. We do not write vape events into Health. A direct Fitbit login stays on this device and is removed from exports.

What this version does not do

We do not show ads, process cards, hold money, or create an account in this version. Cloud sync stays off unless a later owner-approved build connects it. We do not sell your log, and we do not use it for advertising.

Optional local reminders (a daily check-in, or a notice when a pacing window still has unused puffs) stay on this device. We do not send remote or marketing push.

This website

puffpuffstop.com is a static site. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your puff log. The host may keep ordinary connection logs, such as an IP address and the page requested, to run and protect the site.

If you contact us by opening a GitHub issue, the message you write is handled by GitHub under GitHub’s own terms.

Your choices

You can export a JSON copy of your local data or delete everything from Settings. Deleting clears the plan, logs, and settings stored on the phone. An export is a file you control. It can include the local snapshot, and a direct Fitbit login is left out of that file.

The app is not for anyone under 16. Choosing under 16 at the age gate also clears local data and does not start a profile.

How long it is kept

The snapshot remains on the phone until you delete it in Settings, delete the app, or the phone removes the app’s storage. We do not keep a separate server copy of that snapshot in this version.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The app’s in-product privacy screen describes the same local-only practices in shorter form.
