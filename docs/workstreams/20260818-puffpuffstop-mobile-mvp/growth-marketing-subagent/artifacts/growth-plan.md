# Growth plan (no campaigns)

## Positioning

PuffPuffStop helps you tap one Log, keep a daily commitment, and watch cute organs recover as motivational estimates—not a clinic and not a paywalled counter.

- “For kids”, “for children”, Kids Category, under-13
- Clinical diagnosis, proven organ healing, medical device
- Guaranteed quit rates or fabricated user counts
- Wallet / cash-out / real money in v1
- Comparison that clones Puff Count social or IAP

- Paid ads: unauthorized in this workstream
